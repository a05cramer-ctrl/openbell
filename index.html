    03 / MECHANIC

    HOW IT WORKS

    01

    FEES IN

    The engine claims $OPENBELL's pump.fun creator fees (bonding curve and PumpSwap vaults), at most once an hour.

    02

    FIND THE GAP

    Every 60 s it rebuilds this board and takes the deepest discount: gap ≤ −1.50% and ≥ $50K 24h on-chain volume.

    03

    BUY THE DISCOUNT

    It swaps fees into that tokenized stock through Jupiter and stamps the gap in the memo.

    04

    BELL, SELL, BURN

    It sells once on-chain is within 0.30% of the real price, or after the next regular session closes. Profit buys $OPENBELL and burns it.

    WORKED EXAMPLE

    Round numbers for a made-up ticker XYZx, so the maths is easy to follow. Swap fees left out.

    Real close (Fri 16:00 ET)
    $200.00
    XYZx on Solana (Sat)
    $196.00 · 24h volume $50,000 or more
    Gap
    196.00 ÷ 200.00 − 1 = −0.0200 = −2.00% = −200 bps ≤ −150 bps → buy
    Buy
    1.000 SOL of fees → XYZx · memo OPENBELL|BUY|XYZx|-200
    Mon 09:31 ET
    XYZx $199.50 vs real $200.00 → gap −0.25%, inside ±0.30% → sell
    Proceeds
    1.000 SOL × 199.50 ÷ 196.00 = 1.017857142 SOL (SOL/USD unchanged)
    Realized PnL
    1,017,857,142 − 1,000,000,000 = 17,857,142 lamports · memo OPENBELL|SELL|XYZx|17857142
    Burn
    0.017857142 SOL buys $OPENBELL, every token received is burned · memo OPENBELL|BURN|<amount>
    Principal
    1.000 SOL goes back to the fee budget for the next gap

    04 / FIXED

    RULES

    −1.50%

    ENTRY

    Buy only when on-chain trades at least 1.50% below the real price. Deepest gap wins; ties go to more volume.

    $50K

    LIQUIDITY FLOOR

    Minimum 24h on-chain volume (Jupiter buy + sell) for a token to be a target.

    ±0.30%

    GAP-CLOSE EXIT

    Sell when on-chain is back within 0.30% of the real price.

    16:00

    TIME EXIT

    Otherwise sell after the next regular session closes (13:00 ET on early-close days).

    1

    BURN

    PROFIT ONLY

    Realized profit buys back and burns $OPENBELL. A losing trade burns nothing; principal returns to the budget.
